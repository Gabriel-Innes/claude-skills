# TdsTypeEnum (Enumeration)

Withholding tax types.

| Member | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| wtETds | 0 | The withholding tax is of eTDS type. |
| wtGstTds | 1 | The withholding tax is of GST TDS type. |
| wtGstTcs | 2 | The withholding tax is of GST TCS type. |
| wtTcs | 3 | The withholding tax is of TCS type. |
