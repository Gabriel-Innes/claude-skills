# ServiceTypeEnum (Enumeration)

Specify the service type, whether your company provides support services to its customers, or receives support services from your vendors.

| Member | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| srvcSales | 1 | Indicates that the service is provided to your customer. |
| srvcPurchasing | 2 | Indicates that the service is provided by your vendor. |
