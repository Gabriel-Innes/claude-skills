# MultipleCounterRoleEnum (Enumeration)

SAP Business One supports the multiple counters scenarios. Specify the role of the multiple counters.

| Member | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| mcrTeamCounter | 0 | A group of counters' counting results of an item at a storage location add up to its total quantity. |
| mcrIndividualCounter | 1 | Individual counters conduct independent counting of an item at a storage location. |
